<objective>
Remove the built-in Inbox view from the documents view switcher now that the
backend (plan 07-04) stops feeding it rows: the row, its count, its i18n keys,
and — per UI-SPEC section 2 — any persisted workspace filter still pointing at
it, reset silently to All.

rows PERF-06 removes from the index; keeping the view would leave a permanently
empty switcher entry (ROADMAP criterion 4: "gone from the documents view
switcher rather than left permanently empty"). This is a removal-shaped change:
no new screens, components, tokens, or copy. The standalone Inbox pane (app
mode), its pending-item queue, the Files browser, and content search are
different surfaces and stay untouched.

Output: narrowed `BuiltInDocumentView` union and matching logic, three
coordinated switcher/count edits, both locale keys removed, the persisted-filter
fail-open reset, and the two unit-test files updated. Frontend half of PERF-06.
</objective>
