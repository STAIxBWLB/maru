<objective>
Land SEC-01: drop `script-src blob:` from the shipped CSP behind the two proofs D-04
requires. The desktop webview renders third-party content (Telegram, KakaoTalk,
Gmail, Outlook, inbox drops) through innerHTML sinks; while `script-src` allows
blob:, injected DOM can mint executable script URLs (Tampering / Elevation of
Privilege, high, primary trust boundary). Dev serves no CSP at all, so only
packaged-build proofs are valid.

Purpose: make the CSP narrowing a gated, continuously-verified fact rather than a
one-time edit: a static dist scan rides `make verify`, and a packaged-binary scan
rides the `release-checks` window so every future release re-proves it.

Output: `scripts/check-csp-blob.mjs` (two-half guard, drilled red-then-green), its
vitest policy pin, the tauri.conf.json narrowing, the package.json and Makefile
wiring. Implements D-04; respects D-05 (worker-src untouched).
</objective>
