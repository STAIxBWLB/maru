<objective>
Make a failed teardown save visible and recoverable (REL-03): a toast that names the
file and reason and offers to open a recovery copy kept under `.maru/recovery/`
(D-07, D-08), plus the same line in the log.

Purpose: plan 09-05 made teardown paths settle their saves and log a failure; this
plan turns a failure into the user-visible signal REL-03 demands and keeps the edit
as a real file. The toast rides the existing `OperationNotice` queue in
`errorStore.ts` (it survives unmount and does not overwrite a concurrent failure),
and "open" reuses the existing `open_in_file_manager` command, so no IPC surface is
added beyond 09-04's `write_recovery_copy`.

Output: `reportTeardownSaveFailure` wired into the teardown settle, an optional
`recovery` field on `OperationNotice`, the toast extracted into
`src/components/OperationNoticeToast.tsx` with its Open action, i18n keys, and tests.
</objective>
