## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-09-08-02 | Denial of Service | quit blocked indefinitely by a stuck save | medium | mitigate | 3 s budget races the flush; on expiry the user regains control with Retry, Quit anyway, or Cancel |
| T-09-08-03 | Denial of Service | terminals killed by a quit that was then cancelled | medium | mitigate | The guard does not call `window.close()` on failure or timeout, so the Rust exit arm never runs (D-06); guard tests assert `close` is not called |
