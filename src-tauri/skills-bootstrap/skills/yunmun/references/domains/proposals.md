# Domain — 사업계획서·제안서 (proposals and grant applications)

Covers government and foundation grant applications, business plans, project proposals, and statements of work. Run with `professional-pass.md` (article-like weighting) and `languages/ko.md`.

**The call for proposals is the venue corpus.** When the caller supplies or permits them, read the 공고문, the evaluation criteria and their weights, and the required form before anything else. Without them, say so in the report and work from this file's baseline. Section order, headings, page limits and fixed tables come from the form and are never "fixed". Where the form is 개조식, form belongs to `gaejosik`.

## Human baseline

A winning proposal reads as written by people who have done this before: it names their own prior results, their own constraints, and the specific gap this funding closes. Its space is spent in proportion to the evaluation weights. Its targets are numbers someone will be held to.

## AI tells in this domain

| Tell | Fix |
|---|---|
| Interchangeable 필요성: global trends, 4차 산업혁명, AI 시대, policy slogans | The applicant's own situation: the local figure, the gap observed, the prior attempt and its result |
| Differentiation by adjective: `차별화된`, `혁신적인`, `국내 최초`, `세계적 수준` | The concrete difference a reviewer can verify: an asset, a partner, a track record, a number |
| Every strategy a triple with a slogan name | The structure the work actually has |
| Targets without basis: `만족도 90% 이상`, `취업률 80%` | Baseline, basis for the target, how and when it is measured |
| Risk section absent, or risks that are not risks | Two or three real risks with the mitigation already planned |
| Budget narrative that restates the table | The reasoning a reviewer would question: unit price basis, why this item is needed |
| Equal space to every section regardless of evaluation weight | Space follows the scoring table |
| Partner lists without roles | Who does what, confirmed by which document (MOU, letter, contract) |

## Rules

1. **Map every evaluation criterion to a place in the text** where a reviewer finds the answer without searching.
2. **Evidence belongs to the applicant**: own data, own results, own documents. Generic statistics are background at most.
3. **Every target is measurable and owned**: indicator, baseline, target, measurement method, timing.
4. **Never invent results, partners, letters, figures or citations.** Missing material becomes `〔확인필요〕` or a question to the user.
5. **Consistency across sections is protected**: budget totals, headcounts, dates and indicator values must match everywhere; report mismatches rather than silently choosing one.
6. **Persuasion comes from specificity, not intensity.** Delete intensifiers before adding anything.
